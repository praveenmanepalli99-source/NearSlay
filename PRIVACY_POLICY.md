# Privacy Policy for NearFix

**Last Updated:** September 2, 2026

## 1. Introduction
Welcome to **NearFix** ("we", "our", or "us"). NearFix is dedicated to protecting the privacy of our users ("Residents", "Service Providers", or "you"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our NearFix mobile application and related services.

## 2. Information We Collect
We only collect information necessary to connect residents with local colony repair technicians:

1. **Contact & Profile Information:**
   - Name
   - Mobile Phone Number (used for OTP authentication and customer service connections)
   - Profile Picture / Avatar (optional, uploaded with your consent)
   - Flat / Apartment Address & Colony Name (for dispatching repair technicians)
   - Service Category & Hourly Rate (for Service Providers)

2. **Location Data:**
   - **Precise GPS Location (Foreground only):** Collected only when you actively request "Auto-Fill GPS" or during registration to detect your colony/city and calculate proximity (5km / 10km radius) to available technicians.
   - **Background Location:** NearFix **does not** track your location in the background when the app is closed.

3. **Device Information:**
   - Device model, operating system version, and general network connectivity status for app performance and bug prevention.

## 3. How We Use Your Information
We use the collected information for the following purposes:
- Enabling local colony repair requests and technician discovery.
- Routing phone calls to providers via the native dialer.
- Authenticating user accounts via one-time passcodes (OTP).
- Storing your active service status (Available vs. Busy).
- Facilitating resident/provider account management and profile updates.

## 4. Permissions Disclosure (Google Play Compliance)
- **Location (`ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION`, `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION`):** Used solely to identify your neighborhood and match nearby repair technicians within 5km-10km range.
- **Camera & Photos (`READ_MEDIA_IMAGES` / Gallery):** Used solely when you choose to upload or change your profile picture.

## 5. Account Deletion & Data Retention
You have full control over your data. You can permanently delete your account and all associated profile information at any time directly within the NearFix app:
1. Navigate to **My Profile**.
2. Scroll to the **Account Actions** section.
3. Tap **Delete Account Permanently** and confirm.
Upon confirmation, all personal profile records, phone numbers, and directory listings are permanently erased from local and cloud databases.

## 6. Data Security
We implement administrative, technical, and physical security measures to safeguard your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.
