Child Safety Standards & Anti-CSAE Policy

NearSlay Official Safety Standards • Published: September 2026
Strict Zero Tolerance Policy

NearSlay (Package: com.nearfix.app.nearfix) is committed to providing a safe, trusted, and respectful platform for neighborhood services. We strictly prohibit and maintain a zero-tolerance policy against any form of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM), Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE), or any harm directed at minors.

🛡️ Zero Tolerance Statement

Any account found uploading, transmitting, or engaging in child sexual exploitation, abusive content, grooming, or child harm will face immediate, permanent termination and immediate reporting to global law enforcement agencies and child protection organizations.

1. Prohibited Activities

Users and service providers on NearSlay must adhere strictly to the following standards:

  • No sharing, transmitting, displaying, or linking to Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) or any depictions of child exploitation.
  • No predatory behavior, grooming, harassment, or solicitation involving minors.
  • No endangering the welfare, privacy, or safety of children in any way.

2. Content Moderation & Enforcement

  • Immediate Account Termination: Violators will be banned immediately and permanently across all devices and phone numbers.
  • Permanent Data Erasure & Preservation: Abusive content is taken down instantly while evidentiary logs are preserved exclusively for lawful police investigations.
  • Law Enforcement Referral: We cooperate fully with law enforcement, national cybercrime cells, and organizations such as the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and local statutory authorities.
