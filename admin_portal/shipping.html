Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • Mandatory Razorpay Compliance Document

This Shipping and Delivery Policy explains how services and digital subscriptions purchased on NearSlay (and NearFix) are delivered to customers and service partners.

1. Nature of Products & Services

NearSlay is a technology platform offering:

  • Digital Platform Subscriptions: NearSlay Pro monthly membership for independent service providers.
  • Doorstep Hyperlocal Home Services: Physical maintenance and repair works (Electrical, Plumbing, Carpentry, AC Repairs, Cleaning) rendered at residential locations.

2. Delivery of Digital Subscriptions (NearSlay Pro)

Instant Digital Delivery: All digital subscription plans purchased via Razorpay are activated automatically and instantaneously upon payment confirmation.
  • Upon successful payment via UPI, Debit/Credit Card, or NetBanking, your service provider profile is updated in real-time.
  • A digital confirmation with the Tax Invoice and Razorpay Payment ID is displayed on screen and sent to your registered contact information.
  • No physical goods or parcel shipping is involved for digital subscriptions.

3. Delivery of Doorstep Home Services

  • Physical services are delivered by independent neighborhood technicians directly at the resident's home address.
  • The delivery timeline depends on the mutual appointment agreed between the resident and the technician (typically within 30 to 60 minutes for urgent requests, or as scheduled by the resident).
  • Service quality and completion are verified directly on-site by the resident prior to making direct labor payment to the provider.

4. Shipping Costs

Because NearSlay provides digital subscriptions and on-demand doorstep service discovery, there are zero shipping charges or physical handling fees applicable to our platform.
