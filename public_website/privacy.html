NearSlay Privacy Policy

Official Policy for Google Play Store • Last updated: September 2026
Google Play User Data & Privacy Compliance

Welcome to NearSlay ("we", "our", or "us"). NearSlay is a neighborhood service discovery application designed to connect residents with verified local service providers (plumbers, electricians, mechanics, hotels, restaurants, decorators, tent house operators, carpenters, etc.).

This Privacy Policy outlines how NearSlay collects, uses, protects, and discloses personal and device information when you use our Android mobile application and web portal.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information necessary to provide doorstep service connections:

  • Phone Number: Used for secure OTP (One-Time Password) SMS authentication and establishing direct phone communication between residents and technicians.
  • User Profile: Name, optional avatar/profile photo, flat/house number, and colony name.
  • Service Provider Details: Service trade category, hourly rates/charges, colony location, and availability status.
  • Approximate & Precise Location (Foreground only): Used only with your explicit permission when clicking "Auto-Fill GPS" to detect your neighborhood and calculate distance (5 km / 10 km radius) to nearby service providers.

📍 Background Location Policy

NearSlay NEVER tracks your location in the background when the app is closed or minimized. Location data is processed only in the foreground when you actively interact with location features.

2. Android Permissions Used

  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION & ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION: To detect your colony and calculate distance to nearby technicians.
  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / CAMERA: Only requested when you choose to take or upload a custom profile picture.
  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: To communicate with the cloud database and sync service listings.

3. How We Use and Share Information

  • Connecting local residents directly with neighborhood technicians.
  • Enabling real-time direct dialing through your device's native phone dialer.
  • We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third-party advertisers or data brokers.

4. Data Retention & Permanent Account Deletion

You have complete ownership over your account data. In accordance with Google Play's Account Deletion Policy, NearSlay provides instant, permanent self-service account deletion:

  • Open My Profile in the NearSlay app.
  • Scroll to Account Actions.
  • Tap Delete Account Permanently.

Upon confirmation, your profile, contact details, and all registration listings are immediately and permanently erased from both local device storage and the cloud server.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard encryption, HTTPS transmission, and secure tokenization to safeguard all user information against unauthorized access or tampering.
