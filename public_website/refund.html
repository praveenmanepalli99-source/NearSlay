Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • Mandatory Razorpay & RBI Compliance Document

At NearSlay (and NearFix), we believe in transparent, honest business practices. This Refund & Cancellation Policy details the terms governing subscription cancellations and refund requests for digital services purchased via our platform.

1. Subscription Cancellation

  • Service Providers who subscribe to the NearSlay Pro monthly plan (₹99 + 18% GST = ₹116.82/month) may cancel their recurring subscription at any time.
  • To cancel, simply visit My Profile > Subscription Details > Cancel Subscription inside the NearSlay mobile app, or contact our support team at nearfix99@gmail.com.
  • Upon cancellation, your Pro listing benefits remain active until the end of your current paid billing period, and no further automatic renewals will take place.

2. Eligibility for Refunds

Refunds are initiated under the following specific circumstances:

  • Duplicate / Multiple Debits: If your bank account or UPI was charged more than once for a single monthly billing cycle due to a technical glitch or network delay.
  • Payment Deducted but Subscription Not Activated: If money was debited from your account via Razorpay but the NearSlay Pro subscription failed to activate within 2 hours.
  • Cancellation within 7 Days of Initial Billing: If you subscribed to NearSlay Pro and decide within 7 days of activation that the service is not suited for your business, and you have not received customer calls during this window, you may request a 100% full refund.

Refund Processing Timeline

Once a refund request is approved by our billing team, the refund is initiated immediately through the Razorpay Payment Gateway. Refunds are typically credited back to the customer's original payment method (Bank Account, UPI, or Credit/Debit Card) within 5 to 7 business working days, subject to your issuing bank's settlement cycle.

3. Direct Resident & Technician Service Transactions

NearSlay is an open discovery platform. Payments for physical doorstep repairs (plumbing, electrical work, carpentry) are paid directly between the resident and the technician. Any cancellations or refund requests for physical technician labor must be settled mutually between the customer and the respective technician.

4. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund for your subscription, please submit your request along with proof of payment:

  • Email: nearfix99@gmail.com
  • Subject line: Refund Request - [Your Registered Phone Number / Razorpay Payment ID]
  • Details to Include: Razorpay Payment ID (e.g. pay_...), transaction date, amount debited, and reason for refund.
